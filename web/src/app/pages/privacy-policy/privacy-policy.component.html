Privacy policy or terms and conditions

Email and SMS communications

The application may use email and SMS functionality to communicate with users. Any personal data collected for this purpose, including email addresses and phone numbers, is processed solely for application-related communications.

Such data is treated as confidential and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except where required by law or necessary to provide the service in compliance with applicable regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Users may exercise their right to withdraw consent at any time by logging into their account, navigating to the Admin section, and removing the stored email or SMS credentials. Upon removal, the application will cease all related communications.

The application does not charge users for its services. Any charges incurred for SMS messages or emails are determined by the user’s telecommunications or service provider. The application bears no responsibility or liability for such charges.
