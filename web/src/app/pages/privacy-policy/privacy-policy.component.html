Privacy policy

How Health Tracker & PFM (Personal Financial Management) handles personal information, including when you connect financial accounts through Plaid. Contact data is treated as confidential and is not sold or rented for marketing.

Who we are

This policy describes the Health Tracker & PFM application you sign into (“the application”, “we”, “us”). It is a personal productivity and finance companion; operators control deployment and support channels for your environment.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may process categories such as:

  • Account and profile data: username, password (stored only as a one-way hash), optional profile fields you enter (for example name, date of birth, contact email or phone where configured), and sign-in related events where logging is enabled.
  • Application content: health/exercise logs, journal entries, tasks, calendar data, uploaded files you attach, and finance workspace content you create or import.
  • Banking and finance imports: transaction rows and metadata extracted from files you upload or from synchronized feeds described below (including account masks/names surfaced by those feeds).
  • Communications: if you use email or SMS features, addresses or numbers you provide for outbound messages from the application.

Financial data & Plaid (third-party services)

If your deployment enables Plaid, you may connect financial institutions using Plaid Link. Plaid is a separate company; their privacy notice applies when you use Link.

When you connect through Plaid, subject to what Plaid returns for your institution:

  • We may receive identifiers such as Item identifiers (shown in abbreviated form in the UI), institution identifiers, and tokens needed for ongoing synchronization — stored according to deployment configuration (including optional application-level encryption of sensitive tokens).
  • We may retrieve transaction history over HTTPS from Plaid and convert it into the same kind of structured banking data as manual imports (for example via imported ledger rows).
  • Generated interchange files (such as QFX/OFX-style payloads written under the server import directory) may be retained on disk as part of normal import processing unless operators configure retention or cleanup separately.

Consent & acknowledgment: Before starting Plaid Link for the first time in this application, you must acknowledge this section (Financial data & Plaid) so we can record your consent to our collection, processing, and storage of financial connection data as described here and in any deployment-specific notices your administrator provides. Plaid Link itself includes additional disclosures from Plaid and your financial institution.

Disconnecting: You may disconnect Plaid for a banking institution from the Finance → Banking (Admin) tools where offered; that removes our stored Plaid Item credentials for that institution from the application database. It does not automatically delete transactions already imported into your ledger or delete historical import files — use import history removal where available if you want those records deleted.

How we use information

We use personal information to operate features you choose to use: authentication, storing your content, computing summaries and reports you view, sending transactional communications when channels are enabled, and imports or sync you initiate.

We do not sell personal information or rent contact lists for advertising.

Legal bases & regional references

Where regulations such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA apply, different categories of processing may rely on consent (for example optional marketing or messages), contract performance (providing the service you requested), or legitimate interests balanced against your rights — consistent with how your operator documents processing for your tenant.

Retention

Data is kept while your account exists and as needed to provide features you use. Operators may configure backups, logs, and infrastructure retention separately. Banking import files and ledger rows persist until you or an administrator deletes them using in-application tools where supported.

Your choices

  • Email & SMS: Where email or SMS is used for application communications, you may withdraw consent by removing stored credentials or disabling related features under Admin where applicable.
  • Plaid: Disconnect institutions as described above; clear imported data using banking import management where available.
  • Profile: Update profile fields under Admin → My profile subject to any locked contact fields your deployment uses for authentication recovery.

Security

We use industry-standard practices appropriate to the deployment: HTTPS for browser traffic to the API where configured, authenticated APIs, role-based admin boundaries, and documented security operations. See the in-app Information security page for our security program summary.

Children

The application is not directed at children and is intended for adults who operate their own accounts.

Changes

Operators may update this notice when features change (including Plaid or banking capabilities). Material changes are typically communicated through the application or your administrator; continued use after acknowledgment may apply for optional integrations.

Contact

Questions about this policy for your account should go through your administrator’s support channel. Signed-in users may also use Contact Us where enabled.

Email and SMS communications

The application may use email and SMS functionality to communicate with users. Any personal data collected for this purpose, including email addresses and phone numbers, is processed solely for application-related communications.

Such data is treated as confidential and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing, except where required by law or necessary to provide the service in compliance with applicable regulations referenced above.

Users may exercise their right to withdraw consent for these channels by logging into their account, navigating to the Admin section, and removing the stored email or SMS credentials. Upon removal, the application will cease related communications.

The application does not charge users for its services. Any charges incurred for SMS messages or emails are determined by the user’s telecommunications or service provider.
