What you can do here

Each top tab is a workspace. Use them in any order—data is saved as you go.

fitness_center
Exercise Training log & catalog

Pick exercises, log sets and days, and follow strength or weight work over time.
account_balance_wallet
Finance Banking, trading, taxes & alerts

Banking for institutions, file imports, Plaid, and ledger views—with filters by institution or by institution type when you have configured types in Admin. Under Trading, use Screeners for NASDAQ mid-cap names, names near 52-week highs, breakout-style setups, and a quick watchlist headline sweep, plus the new Predicts tab for community sentiment and attention spikes powered by StockTwits + FinBERT—alongside news, alerts, Robinhood summaries, and the market overview tab.

task_alt
Management Tasks, work, travel & balance

Balance categories, calendar, account vault, month notes, and long-form write-ups—plus Work (tasks and daily log) and Travel (trips, map, places, and optional geocode)—all scoped to you.

Capture thoughts with tags and browse your history when you need context.

Reports Summaries & exports

Pull together exercise and journal views. On this page, open the Finance tab and Banking for yearly money-flow summaries—including roll-ups by institution type when your institutions are tagged in Admin → Finance → Banking.

Per-user configuration, your member profile, sign-in history for your account (and full audit if you are an admin). Admins can tune finance notifications: stock-alert email and SMS can use the default AWS path, and outbound mail may use SES or SMTP depending on server settings.

Getting started

  1. 1

    Stay oriented with the tabs

    The bar under the header mirrors major areas of the app. You can return to this Welcome tab anytime for a quick map.

  2. 2

    Log a workout

    Open Exercise, choose an exercise from the catalog (or add one under Admin if you have access), and record today’s session.

  3. 3

    Shape your week

    In Management, create tasks with balance categories and due dates, use the calendar to see what needs attention, and try Travel for trip planning on the map when you want it.

  4. 4

    Optional: Finance, screeners, predicts & journal

    Open Finance for banking (ledger and filters by institution or type), tax helpers, and the Trading tab—try Screeners for curated Yahoo-backed lists (including a NASDAQ mid-cap band) and Predicts for community sentiment (StockTwits + FinBERT) and attention spikes against per-ticker baselines. Configure banking institution types under Admin → Finance → Banking. Use Journal for dated entries and Reports for rollups—including Finance → Banking summaries by type.

  5. 5

    Review account activity

    Under Admin → Sign-in log, you can review recent sign-ins and security-related events for your account.
