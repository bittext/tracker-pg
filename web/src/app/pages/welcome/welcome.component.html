Getting started

  1. 1

    Stay oriented with the tabs

    The bar under the header mirrors major areas of the app. You can return to this Welcome tab anytime for a quick map.

  2. 2

    Log a workout

    Open Exercise, choose an exercise from the catalog (or add one under Admin if you have access), and record today’s session.

  3. 3

    Shape your week

    In Management, create tasks with balance categories and due dates, then use the calendar to see what needs attention.

  4. 4

    Optional: Finance & Journal

    Connect money tracking under Finance when you need it. Use Journal for dated entries and Reports for rollups.

  5. 5

    Review account activity

    Under Admin → Sign-in log, you can review recent sign-ins and security-related events for your account.
