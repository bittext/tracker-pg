Management

Organize to-dos by balance area and urgency, then review them on the calendar. Define categories and task types under Admin → Management.

Track your balance

Assign each task a balance category (work, health, relationships, finance, etc.) so you can see whether attention is spread across what matters-not just what is loudest.

Urgency

Use Low, Medium, and High to separate noise from real deadlines. The calendar highlights days with open work.

Calendar integration

Set a due date to place the to-do on the grid. Tasks without a date stay in Unscheduled until you slot them.
