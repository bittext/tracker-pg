Management

Organize to-dos by balance area and urgency, then review them on the calendar. Define balance categories and task types under Admin → Management (per login); use this page for tasks, the Work hub (work tasks + daily work log), Travel (trips and map pins), Documents (your private uploads with labels), Now (a simple three-column roadmap: planned, in progress, and completed—edit the data file to update), calendar, accounts, month notes, and year-scoped write-ups. Review sign-in activity under Admin → Sign-in log.

Track your balance

Assign each task a balance category (work, health, relationships, finance, etc.) so you can see whether attention is spread across what matters-not just what is loudest.

Urgency

Use Low, Medium, and High to separate noise from real deadlines. The calendar highlights days with open work.

Calendar integration
