Who we are

This policy describes how Health Tracker & PFM (“the application”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information for signed-in users of this deployment.

Email and SMS communications

Health Tracker & PFM may send transactional SMS messages when you explicitly opt in. Typical message types include stock and market event alerts, trading approval requests, account/security one-time passcodes (OTP), and similar application notifications you enable.

How you opt in: In Admin → My profile, check the SMS consent box and save. Finance SMS alerts also require a stored E.164 mobile number under Admin → Finance → Notifications. SMS is never enabled automatically.

Message frequency varies based on the alerts and events you configure. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier.

Help / Opt out: Reply HELP for help, or STOP to cancel SMS. You may also uncheck SMS consent in My profile, disable SMS under Finance → Notifications, or remove your stored mobile number.

Phone numbers and consent records are processed solely to deliver these application communications. We do not sell or rent SMS contact data for third-party marketing. See also our Terms of service.
