# Branding and controls

The agent is supposed to *be* the brand: name, voice, look, and mid-flight content updates.

## Briefing

Studio’s branding block is not decoration. Sierra’s pitch is that the agent represents “the company at its best,” so first impressions and tone are product requirements.

Controls they list:

- **Identity** — name, voice, welcome message.
- **Look** — logo, colors, and related chrome so chat/web does not look like a generic widget.
- **Dynamic updates** — push the latest promotion, outage, or policy change so the agent does not wait for a model retraining or a full release.
- **Multimodality** — upload images and video for product show, troubleshooting steps, and comprehension (not text-only).
- **Channel attachments** — Agent OS 2.0’s maps / fillable forms / charts, intended to work across surfaces.

Tone and style are also first-class in Ghostwriter prompts and in journey guardrails. Chubbies’ public quote is the extreme version: keep a “fun, laid-back spirit” *and* still move the business.

Brand without guardrails is a liability (off-policy jokes, medical advice). Brand without dynamic updates is a stale promo. Treat this module as coupled to [trust](../operations/trust-and-compliance.md) (topic filters) and [knowledge](knowledge.md) (what is allowed to be said).
