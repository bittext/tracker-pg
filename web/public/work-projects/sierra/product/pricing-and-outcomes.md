# Pricing and outcomes

Sierra sells on **outcomes**, not seats or raw conversation volume.

## Briefing

On the product page Sierra says they pioneered **outcome-based pricing**: you pay when the software achieves specific, valuable outcomes. They pair that with a partnership model — an expert agent-development team, “only pay for the value delivered,” deploy in weeks, then grow sophistication.

This is a deliberate contrast to:

- **Per-seat** contact-center software (you pay for humans even when volume drops)
- **Per-message / per-minute** bot pricing (you pay more when the bot is chatty or fails and retries)
- **Implementation-then-abandon** SI projects

The implication for how you *design* an agent: “success” has to be a measurable event (claim filed, return processed, subscription saved, mortgage application started), not “the model produced a reply.” That same definition shows up in Studio **simulations** (evaluate against defined outcomes) and Insights **key metrics** (CSAT, case resolution).

Sierra does not publish a public rate card. Treat any dollar figures you hear in sales conversations as local notes, not catalog facts.
