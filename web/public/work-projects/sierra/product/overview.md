# Product overview

What Sierra is selling: a branded AI agent that *is* the company in conversation, not a generic chatbot bolted onto a help center.

## Briefing

Sierra was co-founded by **Bret Taylor** (ex-Salesforce co-CEO, Quip, Facebook CTO, Google Maps) and **Clay Bavor** (18 years at Google, including Labs, AR/VR, Lens, Workspace product/design). They announced the company publicly in February 2024 as “the conversational AI platform for businesses.”

The product is not a toolkit of widgets. The unit of value is **your company’s agent**: one persona that can reason through a request, stay on brand, take action in systems of record, and hand off to a human with context. Sierra’s own framing: solve the tension between better experience and lower cost by talking to more customers, more often, in more personalized ways.

Named early design partners included **WeightWatchers, SiriusXM, Sonos, and OluKai**. Sierra later cites brands such as Casper, Clear, Minted, Chubbies, and says **40% of the Fortune 50** partner with them. Public launch numbers (Sierra’s own): the WeightWatchers agent handling **almost 70% of customer sessions** at **4.6/5 CSAT**. Clay Bavor’s examples: WW on points and subscriptions; SiriusXM on radio diagnosis and channel find.

Use cases they explicitly name as “finish the job,” not FAQ: process an insurance claim, return an order, originate a mortgage, authenticate a patient, save a subscriber. Verticals they claim at scale: financial services, telecom, tech, healthcare, travel, retail.

The commercial wrapper is a **long-term partnership** plus an expert agent-development team (hundreds of deployments in their telling), not a self-serve widget you abandon after go-live. See [pricing and outcomes](pricing-and-outcomes.md).
